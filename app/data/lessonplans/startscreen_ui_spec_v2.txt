
UI Specification – Start Screen (STEaiM-CT AI Tutor)

Layout

At the top of the screen, the STEaiM-CT-AI logo is displayed centered above the title. 
The logo serves as visual project identification and branding.


Title

Welcome!

The title is large, bold, and dark grey to black. It serves as the entry headline of the interface and signals the beginning of the tutor workflow.


Introductory text (three paragraphs)

Prototype v0.2 of an AI tutor that should give feedback to lesson plans.

Tell the tool your country, subject, the age of the children and upload your lesson plan without mentioning any personal data.

You are welcome to use your native language of course ;)

The text explains the purpose of the tutor, the required inputs, and clarifies that personal data must not be included. 
It also reassures users that they can interact in their preferred language.


Buttons

Below the text, three large horizontal rounded buttons are displayed.
Each button includes:
- an icon on the left
- centered text
- a right arrow indicating navigation to another screen


Button 1 (Primary action)

Text:
I understand and want to proceed

Color:
Blue-to-violet gradient

Icon:
Checkmark inside a circle

Function:
This button starts the tutor workflow.
It navigates to Step 1 of the process where the teacher selects:
- country
- subject
- student age group
and uploads the lesson plan document.

This is the main entry point into the structured lesson plan feedback process.


Button 2

Text:
I need more information

Color:
Pink-to-magenta gradient

Icon:
Document symbol

Function:
This button opens an information screen explaining:
- how the AI tutor analyses lesson plans
- what types of feedback are generated
- how uploaded lesson plans are processed
- how long data is stored
- what users should expect during the workflow

It supports users who want clarification before continuing.


Button 3

Text:
Information about the project

Color:
Orange gradient

Icon:
Information symbol inside a circle

Function:
This button opens a project information page describing:
- the STEaiM-CT project background
- the research context of the tutor
- the goals of the prototype
- the institutions involved
- contact or attribution information

It provides transparency about the origin and purpose of the system.


Container

All elements are placed on a centered light grey card with rounded corners and a subtle shadow.
This card forms the main visual surface of the start screen and separates the interaction area from the background.

About STEaiM-CT

The STEaiM-CT prototype is designed to support teachers in reflecting on didactics, skills, structure, and curriculum alignment in their lesson plans.

It is part of a research-oriented project that explores explainable AI-supported lesson planning in a local, offline-capable environment.

The system is built to be transparent, teacher-friendly, and free from technical or cloud dependencies. It aims to provide trustworthy support for educators without requiring coding expertise.

For more information, please visit our website: www.steaimct.ukf.sk
